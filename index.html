Privacy Policy

Tubik for iOS and Android · version 1.0 · policy version 2 · effective 3 September 2026

Your music, your folders and your settings stay on your device. There is no account and no analytics. Two things do leave it: in the free version Tubik shows ads, which Google selects, and a check that tells the app whether your subscription is active. Premium removes those ads and the requests behind them. Websites you open in the built-in browser carry their own ads and trackers — those are theirs, not ours, and Premium does not touch them.

1. Who is responsible

Tubik is published by Taras Grygorovych Shevchenko, PE. For anything about this policy, write to nrg.ck.ua@gmail.com.

This policy applies to the Tubik mobile app. It does not apply to third-party websites you open inside the app's browser — see section 6.

2. What we collect about you

Nothing reaches us directly. Tubik has no servers of its own, sends us no usage data, no diagnostics and no crash reports, and has no sign-up, no login and no user profile. Two third-party services do receive data, and only because the free version shows ads and the app sells a subscription.

  • Google AdMob, to select and measure the ads. It receives your device advertising identifier — only if you allow tracking when iOS asks — your IP address and the approximate region derived from it, your device model and system version, and which ads you were shown or tapped. If you decline tracking, that identifier is not shared and the ads become non-personalised. Google acts under its own privacy policy. Its consent form, shown in Europe, also mentions precise location, because its advertising partners are permitted to ask for it. Tubik never supplies it: the app requests no location permission at all, so neither it nor the advertising SDK inside it can read where you are.
  • RevenueCat, to tell the app whether your subscription is active. It receives the purchase receipt issued by Apple, an anonymous identifier the app generates for your installation, your device model, system version and store country. It receives no name, no email address and no payment details: your card is handled by Apple, and we never see it. RevenueCat acts under its own privacy policy.

Neither of them receives your music, your file names, your folders, what you played or anything you did in the built-in browser. That never leaves the device. We ourselves see only totals — how many ads were shown, how many people subscribed — never an individual.

With an active Premium subscription no ad is requested at all, so the advertising SDK makes no network calls.

This section is about the ads Tubik itself shows. Ads and trackers belonging to websites you open in the built-in browser are a separate matter and are not ours — see section 6.

4. When Tubik connects to the internet

Only in response to something you do. There is no background telemetry and no periodic "phone home".

  • You open a page in the built-in browser. The request goes to that website. It will see your IP address and whatever your browser sends, exactly as any browser would.
  • You type a search term in the address bar. The query is sent to DuckDuckGo, the app's search provider, and is subject to DuckDuckGo's privacy policy. A typed address is opened directly and is not sent to a search engine.
  • You download a file. Tubik requests that URL from the server that hosts it. That server sees your IP address.
  • The free version shows you an ad. Tubik requests it from Google, which receives what section 2 lists. With an active subscription this does not happen at all.
  • You open the app in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland. The consent form is fetched from Google once, and your answer is then kept on the device.
  • You buy or restore a subscription. The purchase itself goes through Apple; the receipt is then checked with RevenueCat, which answers whether the subscription is active.

None of these requests pass through servers operated by Taras Grygorovych Shevchenko, PE, because there are none. The ad and subscription requests go to Google and to RevenueCat under their own policies; the rest are between you and the site or server you asked for.

5. If you write to us

The Email developer link in the app opens your own mail app with a message pre-filled with the app version and your device model, to save you looking them up. Nothing is sent until you press send. If you do write, we receive your email address, your message and that technical information, and we use it only to answer you. We keep such correspondence no longer than needed to deal with it, and we do not use it for marketing.

6. Websites you open in the built-in browser

The browser inside Tubik is a real browser: it runs the pages you open, including their scripts, cookies, ads and trackers. Those websites are independent of us and are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one. We do not see, receive or control what they collect.

These are their ads and their trackers, not the ones described in section 2. Premium removes the ads Tubik itself shows and changes nothing about the websites you choose to visit.

You can clear everything those sites left behind at any time: Search → ··· → Clear History and Website Data. That removes browsing history, cookies, caches and site storage held by the app.

Tubik does not bypass DRM, paywalls or sign-in, and does not extract streams from YouTube, SoundCloud or similar services. It lists direct audio links that a page already exposes. Import only files you own or have permission to use.

7. Children

Tubik is not directed at children and has no features aimed at them. It does contain a general-purpose web browser and, in the free version, advertising, so a parent or guardian should decide whether the app is appropriate; content on third-party websites is not filtered by us. We do not knowingly collect anything from children and we do not treat the app as child-directed for advertising. Nothing about any user reaches us directly in any case — see section 2.

8. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Delete individual tracks or folders — in Media, using swipe or the ··· menu. Deleting a folder deletes the files inside it, and the app tells you how much will be removed before it does so.
  • Clear cover-art cache — More → Media. Cover art is re-read from file tags when needed, so nothing is permanently lost.
  • Clear browser data — Search → ··· → Clear History and Website Data.
  • Remove everything — delete the app. Because all data is inside the app's own storage, deleting the app deletes all of it. There is nothing left on any server of ours, because there is no server of ours.
  • Change your advertising choices — More → Manage consent, shown in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; and Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on the device itself. A Premium subscription removes the ads altogether.

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK, the GDPR gives you rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. We can only act on data we actually hold, which is limited to correspondence you send us (section 5); the legal basis for handling that is our legitimate interest in answering support requests. Personalised advertising runs on your consent, given in the form described in section 4 and withdrawable at any time; the subscription check runs on the necessity of performing our contract with you. Everything else never leaves your device, so you already control it directly. If you are a California resident: passing your advertising identifier to Google for personalised advertising is sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CCPA/CPRA. Declining the tracking request, or subscribing to Premium, stops it. We do not sell personal information for money.

9. Security

Your files and library data live in the app's private storage, protected by the operating system's sandbox and by your device passcode and encryption. Because nothing is transmitted to us, there is no server-side copy to be breached. Network requests to websites and download servers use HTTPS wherever the remote server offers it.

10. Diagnostics shared through Apple or Google

Independently of Tubik, iOS and Android can send crash diagnostics to app developers if you turned that sharing on in your operating system settings. This is handled by Apple or Google under their own policies, not by us. Such reports contain technical crash information, and we may view them in App Store Connect or Google Play Console. You can turn that sharing off in your device settings.

11. Changes to this policy

This is version 2 of this policy. Version 1 said that Tubik collected nothing; that stopped being true when advertising and subscriptions were added, and sections 2, 3, 4 and 8 were rewritten for it. If anything else changes — crash reporting, for instance — this policy will be updated before the version that changes it is released, the App Store privacy information will be updated with it, and material changes will be noted in the release notes. The effective date at the top shows when this version took effect.

4. Коли Tubik виходить у мережу

Лише у відповідь на вашу дію. Немає фонової телеметрії й немає періодичних звернень «додому».

  • Ви відкриваєте сторінку у вбудованому браузері. Запит іде на цей сайт. Він бачить вашу IP-адресу й те, що зазвичай надсилає браузер — так само, як у будь-якому браузері.
  • Ви вводите пошуковий запит в адресний рядок. Запит надсилається до DuckDuckGo — пошуковика застосунку — і підпадає під політику конфіденційності DuckDuckGo. Введена адреса відкривається напряму й до пошуковика не надсилається.
  • Ви завантажуєте файл. Tubik звертається за цим посиланням до сервера, який його роздає. Той сервер бачить вашу IP-адресу.
  • Безкоштовна версія показує вам рекламу. Tubik запитує її в Google, і той отримує перелічене в розділі 2. З чинною підпискою цього не відбувається взагалі.
  • Ви відкриваєте застосунок у ЄЕЗ, Великій Британії чи Швейцарії. Форма згоди один раз завантажується від Google, а ваша відповідь далі зберігається на пристрої.
  • Ви купуєте або відновлюєте підписку. Сама покупка проходить через Apple; далі чек перевіряється в RevenueCat, і той відповідає, чи підписка чинна.

Жоден із цих запитів не проходить через сервери Taras Grygorovych Shevchenko, PE, бо їх немає. Запити реклами й підписки йдуть до Google і до RevenueCat за їхніми політиками; решта — між вами й тим сайтом чи сервером, який ви самі попросили.
